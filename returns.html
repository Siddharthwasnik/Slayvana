Return Eligibility

To be eligible for a return, your item must be:

  • Returned within 7 days of the delivery date
  • Unworn and unwashed, in original condition
  • All original tags attached and packaging intact
  • Free from perfume, deodorant, or stains
  • Accompanied by your order number or invoice

Items not eligible for return:

  • Items marked as "Final Sale" or purchased during clearance events
  • Customised or personalised items
  • Items damaged due to misuse or improper care
  • Innerwear, accessories, or hygiene-sensitive items

How to Initiate a Return

  1. 1

    Go to your My Account page and find the order you wish to return.

  2. 2

    Click "Return / Exchange" and select the item(s) and reason for return.

  3. 3

    Our team will approve the request within 24 hours and email you a return shipping label.

  4. 4

    Pack the item securely in its original packaging and hand it over to the courier.

  5. 5

    Once received and inspected, your refund or exchange will be processed within 3–5 business days.

Alternatively, email us at returns@slayvana.com with your order number and return reason.

Refund Process

Refunds are issued to the original payment method:

Payment MethodRefund Timeline
UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm)1–3 business days
Credit / Debit Card5–7 business days
Net Banking3–5 business days
Cash on DeliveryRefunded to bank account / UPI (3–5 days)

Shipping charges (Rs. 99) are non-refundable unless the return is due to a defective or incorrect item. For exchanges, return shipping is free.

Defective or Wrong Items

If you received a defective product or the wrong item, we sincerely apologise. Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item. We will:

  • Arrange a free return pickup from your doorstep
  • Ship a replacement at no extra cost (if available)
  • Or issue a full refund including original shipping charges
