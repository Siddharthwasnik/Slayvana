Orders & Shipping

Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days across India. Express delivery (1–2 days) is available for select pincodes at checkout for an additional charge of Rs. 99.
Yes! We offer free standard delivery on all orders above Rs. 999. For orders below Rs. 999, a flat shipping fee of Rs. 99 applies.
Once your order is shipped, you'll receive an email and SMS with your tracking number. You can also track your order in real-time from your My Account page.
Orders can be cancelled within 2 hours of placing them. After that, the order may already be processed. Please contact us immediately if you need to cancel.
Currently, Slayvana ships only within India. We are working on international shipping and hope to launch it soon. Sign up for our newsletter to be the first to know!

Returns & Exchanges

We offer a 7-day hassle-free return policy from the date of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original packaging with all tags attached. See our full Returns & Exchanges policy.
Go to your My Account page, find the order, and click "Return / Exchange". Alternatively, email us at returns@slayvana.com with your order number.
Once we receive and inspect the returned item, refunds are processed within 3–5 business days to your original payment method. UPI refunds are typically faster than card refunds.
Yes! Size exchanges are free of charge. Initiate an exchange request within 7 days. The replacement item will be shipped once we receive your return.

Sizing & Fit

Measure your bust, waist, and hips with a tape measure and compare with our Size Guide. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for comfort.
Most of our styles run true to size. However, peplum tops and kurtis have a slightly relaxed fit. Check individual product pages for model measurements and fit notes.
We carry sizes XS to 3XL (equivalent to Indian sizes 28–52). Some styles may not be available in all sizes — check the product page for current availability.

Payments

We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit and Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), Net Banking (all major Indian banks), and Cash on Delivery (COD).
Absolutely. All transactions are SSL encrypted and processed through RazorPay — a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. We never store your card details.

Products & Care

We primarily use GOTS-certified 100% organic cotton, Georgette, Rayon, and Silk blends. Fabric details are mentioned on each product page.
Machine wash cold on gentle cycle. Do not bleach. Iron on medium heat from the inside. For printed pieces, turn inside-out before washing to preserve print quality. Full care instructions are on the product tag.
We use low-impact, azo-free dyes that are tested for colorfastness. Some vibrant colours (like deep reds and navies) may experience minimal bleeding in the first wash — we recommend washing separately the first time.
