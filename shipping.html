Our Shipping Partners

We partner with Delhivery, Blue Dart, Xpressbees, and India Post for reliable pan-India coverage. The courier partner is selected based on your pincode for fastest delivery.

Tracking Your Order

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive:

  • An SMS with your tracking number and carrier link
  • An email confirmation with your invoice and tracking details
  • A WhatsApp notification (if opted in)

You can also track your order anytime from your My Account page.

Address & Delivery Issues

Please ensure your delivery address is complete and correct. Slayvana is not responsible for failed deliveries due to incorrect address information. If your package is returned to us, we will contact you to reship — a redelivery charge may apply.

For any delivery issues, please contact our support team within 48 hours of the expected delivery date.
