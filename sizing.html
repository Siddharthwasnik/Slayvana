Perfect Fit

Size Guide

Find your perfect fit in 3 easy steps.

How to Measure

Use a flexible measuring tape. Measure your body (not over clothing) while standing upright.

👗
1

Bust

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape horizontal.

📐
2

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline — the narrowest part of your torso.

📏
3

Hips

Measure around the fullest part of your hips, about 20cm below your waist.
