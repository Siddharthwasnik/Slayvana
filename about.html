Our Story

Fashion that celebrates
your roots and your rise.

We believe Indian women deserve clothes that are as bold, layered, and multidimensional as they are. Slayvana was born from that belief.

Our Mission

Effortless comfort meets cultural pride.

Slayvana is a modern ethnic fashion brand built for the woman who walks with confidence — whether she's heading to a pooja or a party. We blend the heritage of Indian textiles with the energy of contemporary silhouettes, creating clothes you'll reach for again and again.

Every piece is thoughtfully designed in-house by our team of Indian designers who understand the nuance of dressing for Indian climates, body types, and occasions.

How It All Started

From a Mumbai apartment to 50,000+ happy customers.

Slayvana started in 2022 when founder Aarav Mehta noticed a gap: Indian women wanted clothes that were rooted in tradition but styled for today. He partnered with textile designers from Jaipur and Surat to create the first collection of 12 kurtis — and sold out in 48 hours.

Today, Slayvana ships to 800+ cities across India, employs 40 people, and releases new collections every Friday. The Mumbai apartment has been replaced by a studio, but the spirit remains the same: clothes that make Indian women feel unstoppable.
