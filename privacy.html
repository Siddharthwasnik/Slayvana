Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated: 10 October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you shop at Slayvana, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and password (hashed)
  • Order information: delivery address, order history, payment method type (not card details)
  • Usage data: pages visited, products viewed, time spent, click patterns (via cookies)
  • Device data: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL
  • Communications: messages sent to our support team, reviews submitted

We do not store credit or debit card numbers. All payment processing is handled by RazorPay, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateway.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and fulfil your orders, including shipping and delivery tracking
  • Send order confirmation, shipping updates, and invoices
  • Respond to your customer support queries
  • Personalise your shopping experience (product recommendations)
  • Send promotional emails and offers (with your consent — you can unsubscribe anytime)
  • Improve our website, products, and services through analytics
  • Prevent fraud and ensure platform security
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Information Sharing

We share your personal information only with trusted third parties necessary to operate our business:

  • Delivery partners (Delhivery, Blue Dart, Xpressbees): name, phone, and delivery address for order fulfilment
  • Payment gateway (RazorPay): for processing payments securely
  • Marketing tools (Klaviyo, Meta Pixel): anonymised usage data for personalised ads — you can opt out
  • Analytics (Google Analytics): anonymised, aggregated usage data

We never sell your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including SSL/TLS encryption, secure servers hosted on AWS (Mumbai region), regular security audits, and access controls. Despite these measures, no system is 100% secure — we will notify you promptly in the event of a data breach that affects your personal information.

Your account is protected by a hashed password. Never share your password with anyone, including our support team who will never ask for it.

5. Cookies

We use cookies to improve your experience — keeping you logged in, remembering your cart, and understanding how you use our site. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time. See our full Cookie Policy.

6. Your Rights

Under Indian data protection law (IT Act 2000 and DPDP Act 2023), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your personal data (right to be forgotten)
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@slayvana.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or requests:

  • Email: privacy@slayvana.com
  • Address: Slayvana Fashion Pvt. Ltd., 4B Sunshine Towers, Bandra West, Mumbai — 400050, Maharashtra, India
