Cookie Policy

How we use cookies and how you can manage them.

Last updated: 10 October 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and understand how you use the site. Cookies do not contain your personal information unless you provide it.

Types of Cookies We Use

🔒 Strictly Necessary Cookies

Always On

Essential for the website to function. These cookies enable core features like your shopping cart, account login, and secure checkout. They cannot be disabled.

Examples: session token, cart ID, CSRF protection token

📊 Analytics Cookies

Optional

Help us understand how visitors interact with our site — which pages are popular, where traffic comes from, and how to improve your experience. Data is anonymised and aggregated.

Provider: Google Analytics 4  ·  Retention: 26 months

🎯 Marketing & Personalisation Cookies

Optional

Used to show you relevant ads on other platforms (like Instagram and Facebook) and to personalise product recommendations based on your browsing.

Providers: Meta Pixel, Google Ads  ·  Retention: 90 days

⚙ Functional Cookies

Optional

Remember your preferences such as selected size filters, preferred currency display, and recently viewed products to enhance your shopping experience.

Examples: recently-viewed, size-filter, ui-theme  ·  Retention: 30 days

✓ Your cookie preferences have been saved.

How to Manage Cookies in Your Browser

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Note that blocking all cookies may prevent some features from working correctly.

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge
